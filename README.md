# The Business Problem
Olist is an e-commerce platform that commenced operations as a startup in 2015 with its marketplace spanning a wide range of products including electronics, household goods, and fashion items. The datasets provide snapshots of its early sales activity between 2016 and 2018, offering a valuable opportunity to examine the business during its initial stages of growth. Because the data captures a relatively early period of operations, the analysis focuses on two key questions that are particularly relevant to a growing e-commerce business:

1. What did overall sales performance look like over this period and what benchmarks can be established for tracking future performance?
2. Where are the clearest opportunities for growth based on product categories, customers, and order compositions?
